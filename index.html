Common Questions

BEFORE YOU BOOK

What is the difference between the Minor, Major and Executive services?

The Minor Service covers routine maintenance — the essential oil and filter work with a diagnostic scan. The Major Service adds the air, fuel and cabin filters plus a brake fluid flush, and is the one most vehicles need annually. The Executive Service is the all-inclusive package, adding spark plugs, wheel alignment, balancing and wipers.

How often should I service my car?

It depends on mileage, age and what the manufacturer specifies. As a general guide, a Minor Service suits roughly every 10,000 km, and a Major Service annually or every 15,000 to 20,000 km. Tell us your make and model and we will give you the specific interval.

Do you service all makes and models?

Yes. Our technicians work across passenger vehicles, luxury and European marques, and commercial vehicles, using diagnostic equipment suited to each. We are an independent specialist rather than a franchised dealer.

Will servicing here affect my vehicle warranty?

As an RMI-accredited workshop working to manufacturer specification with quality parts, servicing with us generally keeps your warranty intact. If you have a specific concern about your vehicle, raise it when you book and we will confirm before any work starts.

Are the prices fixed, or should I expect additional charges?

The prices shown are upfront for the inclusions listed, and vary with the make and model of your vehicle. If we identify anything further during the service, you receive a detailed quote and we proceed only with your approval. Nothing is ever added without you knowing first.

Can I have my car serviced overnight?

Yes — every package can be carried out overnight. Drop your vehicle off in the evening and collect it the next morning, serviced and road tested. Let us know when you book so we can hold the slot.

When Something Is Wrong

REPAIRS

Diagnose first. Quote second. Repair third.

All makes and models · Every repair in house

We diagnose first, quote second and repair third. That order matters: replacing parts until a fault disappears is the most expensive way to fix a car, and it is not how we work.

BRAKING SYSTEMS

Pads, rotors, calipers, lines and fluid. Braking is the one system where a marginal saving is never worth making, so we quote on what the measurements say rather than what the customer hoped to hear.

Discs are measured, not eyeballed, and worn parts are kept for you to inspect.

Typical Work

  • Pad and rotor replacement
  • Caliper overhaul
  • Brake fluid flush
  • ABS fault diagnosis
  • Handbrake adjustment

SUSPENSION & STEERING

Shocks, struts, bushes, ball joints and steering components. Worn suspension does not just ride badly — it lengthens stopping distances and wears tyres unevenly, so it is a safety item as much as a comfort one.

Wheel balancing available alongside any suspension work.

Typical Work

  • Shock and strut replacement
  • Bushes and mountings
  • Ball joints and tie rods
  • Steering rack diagnosis
  • Tyres and wheel balancing

ENGINE REBUILDS

Full strip, inspection and rebuild to factory specification. Components are measured against tolerance, machined where appropriate and replaced where not.

A rebuild is a considered decision, so we give you the honest comparison against a replacement unit before any work starts.

Typical Work

  • Full engine strip and rebuild
  • Head skim and gasket work
  • Timing belt and chain
  • Overheating diagnosis
  • Oil leak tracing and repair

AIR-CONDITIONING

Re-gas, leak detection and component repair. A system that needs regassing twice in a year has a leak, and we find it rather than selling you gas again.

Because our electrical work is in house, an air-conditioning fault that turns out to be electrical stays with us.

Typical Work

  • Service and re-gas
  • Leak detection
  • Compressor and condenser
  • Pipes, seals and valves
  • Climate control diagnosis

ELECTRICAL & DIAGNOSTICS

Intermittent faults, warning lights, starting problems, sensors and wiring. This is the work most workshops send away — we do it here, which is why the diagnosis does not get lost in translation.

Every diagnostic session ends with a written explanation of what was found and what it means.

Typical Work

  • ECU and computer diagnostics
  • Wiring and loom repair
  • Sensor testing and replacement
  • Starting and charging faults
  • Lighting and accessories
