What We Do

OUR SERVICES

Four disciplines. One workshop. Every job done in house.

Mechanical · Diagnostics · Preventative · Premium

Four disciplines, one workshop. Whether your vehicle is due a scheduled service, carrying an intermittent fault, or booked in for a rebuild, the same standard of diagnosis and reporting applies.

MECHANICAL REPAIR

Engine, drivetrain, braking, steering and suspension work carried out by technicians who rebuild engines as a matter of routine rather than as an exception.

Every mechanical job begins with an inspection and ends with a road test. Parts are quoted before fitting, and worn components are kept for you to see.

See repair detail

Includes

  • Engine repairs and full rebuilds
  • Brake pads, rotors and fluid
  • Shocks, struts and suspension
  • Clutch, gearbox and drivetrain
  • Cooling systems and overheating

ADVANCED DIAGNOSTICS

Warning lights are symptoms, not diagnoses. We read the fault codes, then test the system properly to establish what is actually causing them before anything is replaced.

Auto-electrical work is done in house, which matters — it is the area most often sent away, and the area where a handover loses the most information.

Book a diagnostic

Includes

  • Computer and ECU diagnostics
  • Electrical fault finding
  • Sensor and wiring diagnosis
  • Full vehicle health checks
  • Written diagnostic report

PREVENTATIVE CARE

The least expensive repair is the one that never became necessary. Scheduled servicing keeps a vehicle within its maintenance intervals and catches wear while it is still cheap to address.

Service history is documented, so your vehicle keeps its record and its value.

Service packages from R850. Minor, Major and Executive options, each available overnight.

Includes

  • Minor and major services
  • Fluids, filters and oil
  • Air-conditioning service and re-gas
  • Tyres and wheel balancing
  • Battery testing
